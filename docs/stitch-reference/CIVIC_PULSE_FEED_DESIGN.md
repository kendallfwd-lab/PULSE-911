## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-clarity, urgent yet poised public-interest social feed. The system merges the curated, feed-first ergonomics of modern desktop social interfaces with the high-stakes legibility demanded by civic alerts, neighborhood coordination, and emergency response.

The emotional baseline is responsive, vigilant, and community-centered. The interface maintains visual quietude—allowing community reporting, multimedia documentation, and civic alerts to command immediate comprehension without ambient visual fatigue.

The aesthetic fuses contemporary **Minimalism** with structural **Low-Contrast Outlines** and crisp micro-elevations. Interactions are direct and lightweight. Surfaces prioritize pure optical whites, subtle cool slate structural boundaries, and high-impact emergency accents that punctuate the screen solely when community intervention or critical safety is invoked.

## Colors

The palette balances clean information hierarchy with immediate urgency signals. 

- **Primary (`#FF2E4D`)**: Vivid coral red designated for high-priority civic notices, SOS beacons, emergency action buttons, live community broadcasts, and critical state markers. It remains strictly selective to retain uncompromised visual alarm.
- **Secondary (`#0EA5E9`)**: Clear ocean cyan used for standard interactive links, verified municipal badges, active navigation indicators, and informational progress counters.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Resilient emerald green for resolved incidents, public utility confirmations, mutual aid matches, and safe community statuses.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Structural slate neutral governing secondary metadata, auxiliary text, inactive icons, and divider lines.

### Surface Tones & Tokens
- **Canvas Base**: `#FFFFFF` for primary feed viewports, modal panels, and content cards.
- **Surface Dim / Side Rail**: `#F8FAFC` for secondary sidebars, trending civic modules, and persistent left-hand navigation.
- **Surface Container High**: `#F1F5F9` for nested comments, subtle hover states, chips, and inactive form controls.
- **Borders & Dividers**: `#E2E8F0` for structural feed framing, item separations, and input borders.

### Text Contrast
- **Text Primary**: `#0F172A` providing crisp, razor-sharp legibility across all editorial and critical alert tiers.
- **Text Secondary**: `#64748B` providing soft yet compliant hierarchy for timestamps, location tags, and metrics.

## Typography

The type system is built on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering geometric clarity, distinct apertures, and contemporary polish. 

- **Display & Headlines**: Tightly tracked with negative letter-spacing to command authority in critical event titles, banner metrics, and modal dialogs.
- **Titles**: Set at medium-to-semibold weights to cleanly separate account handles, incident names, and location pins from body narrative.
- **Body**: Uses open leading (1.5x) to maintain quick skimming and legibility across rapid field reporting, user updates, and complex community bulletins.
- **Labels & Badges**: Employs uppercase or elevated tracking on smaller tokens (`label-sm`) for verification markers, time-elapsed tags, and SOS status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the structural precision of a desktop-first social feed, engineered for scanability, peripheral situational awareness, and media immersion.

### Desktop Three-Column Model (Breakpoint >= 1200px)
- **Left Navigation Rail**: Fixed width (244px to 280px) anchored with clean borders, home navigation, incident filters, and notification badges.
- **Central Feed**: Constrained to a max width of 630px, centering incident media, community posts, and alert bulletins.
- **Right Utility Panel**: Contextual width (320px to 380px) containing local trending reports, active regional radar maps, emergency contacts, and active civic dispatches.

### Responsive Reflow Rules
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: The left rail collapses into an icon-only slim dock (72px). The right utility panel collapses below the primary stream or tucks into an expandable overlay; the main feed maintains its 630px center stage.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: The interface switches to a single fluid column with a fixed top brand bar and bottom utility navigation dock. Gutter reduces to `space-md`, and side margins tighten to `space-sm` for borderless media presentation.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system relies primarily on **Low-Contrast Outlines** complemented by soft, diffused **Ambient Shadows** to preserve an uncluttered light-mode desktop environment.

### Surface Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or subtle canvas tint (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow. Separation achieved via 1px crisp borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Feed Cards & Modules)**: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 0 0 1px #E2E8F0`. Provides defined tactile presence without heavy drop-offs.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Dropdowns & Menus)**: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Suspended cleanly over active media.
- **Level 3 (SOS Flash Banners & Critical Alerts)**: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 46, 77, 0.16), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 46, 77, 0.2)`. Uses a warm, tinted coral aura to demand immediate focus.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Media Viewers)**: Backdrop blur of `8px` with semi-translucent veil (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.5)`), pairing the modal body with `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** shape architecture, balancing friendly community accessibility with technical civic precision.

- **Base Radius (8px / `0.5rem`)**: Applied to post cards, inline media enclosures, standard text fields, search containers, and menu list wrappers.
- **Large Radius (16px / `1rem`)**: Applied to dialog overlays, expanded broadcast drawers, interactive civic voting cards, and modal corners.
- **Extra Large Radius (24px / `1.5rem`)**: Applied to standalone alert banners and visual feature badges.
- **Pill Radius (Full / `9999px`)**: Reserved for user profile avatars, primary calls-to-action ("Follow", "Respond", "Broadcast"), filter chips, and live status badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary / Critical SOS**: Pill-shaped or 8px rounded corners. Solid `#FF2E4D` fill with white text, font `label-lg`, height 40px (desktop) or 44px (touch). Focused state displays a 2px offset ring in `#FF2E4D`.
- **Secondary / Standard**: Pure white background with 1px border in `#E2E8F0` and `#0F172A` text. Hover transitions to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Borderless, padding-confined, `#64748B` text transitioning to `#0F172A` on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips**: 28px height, full pill shape. Active civic alerts render with soft coral tint background (`rgba(255, 46, 77, 0.08)`), text `#FF2E4D`, and optional pulsing dot indicator.
- **Filter Tags**: 32px height, `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` text. Selected state switches to solid `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Feed Cards (Post & Alert Units)
- **Container**: Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 8px rounded corners.
- **Header**: Height 56px, aligning avatar (38px circle), author handle, geographic location pin, and secondary timestamp.
- **Media Frame**: Edge-to-edge within the card horizontal plane with clean 1px bottom border `#F1F5F9`.
- **Action Bar**: Row of clean glyph icons (Heart/Acknowledge, Direct Dispatch, Share, Bookmark) sized at 24px with 16px horizontal spacing.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Desktop Search**: Pill-shaped or 8px rounded `#F1F5F9` container, height 38px, icon prefixed (`#64748B`), no initial border. On focus, shifts to `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#0EA5E9`, and subtle ring.
- **Form Inputs**: 44px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, typography `body-md`. Placeholder in `#94A3B8`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox**: 18px rounded square (4px radius), 1.5px border `#CBD5E1`. Checked state: solid `#0F172A` with white checkmark.
- **SOS Critical Check**: Checked state transitions to solid `#FF2E4D` for required emergency acknowledgments.
- **Radio Buttons**: 18px circular frame with inset dot when selected.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **List Items**: Divided by hairline dividers (`#F1F5F9`). Horizontal hover states display `#F8FAFC` spanning edge-to-edge.
- **Emergency Incident List**: Prefixed by a high-contrast vertical indicator bar (3px width) in `#FF2E4D` along the left border.
